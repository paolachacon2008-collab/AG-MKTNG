Our story

In Arabic, coffee is called qahwa.

In many homes across the Middle East, qahwa is the first thing offered to anyone who arrives: a welcome before any conversation.

QAWA was born from that idea. We took the word that gave coffee its name and brought it to the heart of Málaga, to welcome everyone who walks in the same way: unhurried, with specialty coffee, a matcha or something sweet, freshly made.
